Uber operates Cassandra as a managed service across hundreds of clusters and tens of thousands of nodes, serving tens of millions of queries per second. The article describes its Odin-based lifecycle management, integrated clients, service discovery, and debugging of production scaling problems. Incremental operational changes addressed reliability issues across workloads with different read and write patterns.
