YouTube Music uses Transformers to interpret sequences of listening actions in the user's current context. Instead of treating every historical skip or like as equally relevant, the ranking model learns which earlier actions matter for the present recommendation. The approach integrates sequential user history into the ranking stage of a retrieval, ranking, and filtering pipeline.
