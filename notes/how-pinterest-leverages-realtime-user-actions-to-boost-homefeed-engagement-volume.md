Pinterest added a transformer-based model of the latest 100 user actions to its Homefeed ranker to capture short-term interests alongside longer-term user embeddings. GPU serving kept latency and cost neutral despite the substantially greater model complexity. In an online experiment, the initial model increased overall repin volume by 6% and non-core users' repins by 11%, while reducing hides by 10%; a subsequent model added another 5% repin gain.
