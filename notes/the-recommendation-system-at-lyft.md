Lyft evolved ride-mode recommendations from static price or ETA ordering to personalized models that consider rider history, preferences, and marketplace supply and demand. LightGBM propensity models rank and preselect options, while post-processing supports new modes and additional business goals. Machine learning handles about 90% of use cases, with adjustment layers and selective cross-sells helping balance rider needs and marketplace conditions.
