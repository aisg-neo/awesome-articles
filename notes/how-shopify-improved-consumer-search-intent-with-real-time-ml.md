Shopify built near-real-time text and image embedding pipelines so storefront search can reflect merchant product updates promptly. Dataflow processes about 2,500 embeddings per second, or 216 million per day, with GPU inference. Reducing worker thread concurrency cut memory usage from roughly 104 GB to 40 GB and allowed the team to eliminate a 14% higher-memory-machine cost increase.
