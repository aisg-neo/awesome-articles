Netflix built a media understanding platform to expose machine-learning-derived information about video, audio, images, and text to creative tools. Its Marken service standardizes storage and querying of algorithm outputs, while execution and ingestion tooling indexes generated metadata. The platform enabled search and discovery capabilities to be integrated into several applications without bespoke integrations for every algorithm.
