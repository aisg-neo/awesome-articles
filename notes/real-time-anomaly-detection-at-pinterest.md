Pinterest built Warden as a modular anomaly detection platform with separate data-query, detection, and notification components. It monitors machine learning score drift by comparing current and historical distributions using Population Stability Index. The platform also helps Trust and Safety detect unusual spam activity and identify important segments for further investigation.
