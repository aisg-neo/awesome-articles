LinkedIn replaced separate My Network endpoints with one paginated API, cached recommendation cohorts, and a shared render model to address slow loading and content shifting on screen. The change reduced 90th-percentile latency by 43% and web Largest Contentful Paint by 12%, while cutting first input delay by 90%. It also reduced serving costs by seven figures.
