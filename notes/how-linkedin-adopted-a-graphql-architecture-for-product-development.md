LinkedIn adopted GraphQL after its proprietary Deco query language became difficult to validate and maintain across frontend teams. It generates GraphQL schemas from existing Rest.li definitions and integrates query execution into its established microservices infrastructure. The initial rollout focused on read operations for frontend applications to improve tooling and developer productivity without replacing the underlying services.
