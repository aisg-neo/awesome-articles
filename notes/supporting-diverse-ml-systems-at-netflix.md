Netflix describes how its machine learning platform integrates Metaflow with company-wide data, compute, orchestration, and deployment systems. Fast Data provides efficient Iceberg and Parquet access, while Titus and other production integrations let practitioners scale and operate varied workflows. Hundreds of internal projects use the shared foundation while adding domain-specific libraries and deployment paths where needed.
