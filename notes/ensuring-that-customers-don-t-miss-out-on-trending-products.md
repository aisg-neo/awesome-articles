Amazon developed TrendRec to predict products gaining popularity instead of recommending only those already popular. The model first learns product representations through next-item recommendation, then fine-tunes them to predict acceleration in customer interactions. It beat a baseline neural model across five datasets, with improvements of 15% and 16% on two metrics for one dataset.
