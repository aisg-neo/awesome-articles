Instagram designed Suggested Posts to extend the home feed with content from accounts a user does not follow. It generates candidates from interest seeds using account embeddings and co-occurrence patterns, then ranks them for relevance and engagement. The article also explains how the system handles users with little activity while aiming to make recommendations resemble the user’s existing feed.
