LinkedIn's lakehouse users had to coordinate compute engines, storage, and table catalogs separately. OpenHouse provides a unified control plane where developers declare table definitions and policies while the platform manages tables and their lifecycle. The article explains its use with LinkedIn's managed Iceberg lakehouse to simplify governance and data management.
