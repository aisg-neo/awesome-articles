Snapchat must select ads from millions of candidates under strict cost and latency limits. Its system filters eligibility, retrieves candidates with lightweight models, scores them with heavier models, and runs an auction using predictions, bids, budgets, and business rules. The article describes challenges including auction-induced selection bias and prediction calibration at a scale of trillions of predictions per day.
