Snap built embedding-based retrieval for Spotlight because scoring every video was too expensive and sparse video text limited traditional search. Two-tower models independently encode users and stories using engagement, creator, and content features. The resulting embeddings supply personalized candidates to the ranking pipeline while meeting online latency requirements.
