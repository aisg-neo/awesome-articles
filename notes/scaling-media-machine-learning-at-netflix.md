Netflix built shared media machine learning infrastructure to simplify access to video, audio, images, and text and reduce repeated feature computation. Jasper standardizes media access, Amber stores and computes reusable features, and a Ray-based GPU cluster supports distributed training. Data-loading and preprocessing optimizations increased training throughput three to five times, and the article illustrates the infrastructure through a match-cutting pipeline.
