  - AI/ML
year: 2020
---

Zomato models food preparation time using both the items in an order and restaurant characteristics such as kitchen capacity and queued orders. It combines learned representations of item information with restaurant-level signals and improves training labels through a Food Order Ready button in the partner app. Initial results showed a 9% improvement in predictions falling within five minutes of the actual preparation time.
