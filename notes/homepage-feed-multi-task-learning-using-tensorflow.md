LinkedIn replaced separate feed engagement models with a TensorFlow multi-task learning architecture that shares learning across related behaviors. Two towers separate passive consumption from active contribution objectives, while XGBoost leaf embeddings and inference optimizations control serving costs. The article explains the calibration, training variance, and feature normalization challenges involved in deploying this setup.
