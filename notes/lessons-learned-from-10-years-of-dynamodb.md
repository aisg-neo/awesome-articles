The DynamoDB team describes how a decade of operating the service changed its approach to predictable performance, partitioning, and durability. Changes include a routing-metadata service with constant background traffic, traffic-aware capacity management, global admission control, and continuous verification of stored replicas against archived logs. The article also explains failure testing and customer-facing availability monitoring.
