Lyft uses graph learning to summarize interactions among riders, drivers, locations, and time as compact embeddings. Graphs built from ride histories produce vectors that make relationships and similarities easier to compare than large sparse feature sets. The article demonstrates how these representations reveal geographic and temporal patterns in rideshare behavior.
