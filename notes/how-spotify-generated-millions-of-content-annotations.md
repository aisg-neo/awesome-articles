Spotify needed training and evaluation annotations for catalogs containing hundreds of millions of tracks and podcast episodes. It built an annotation platform combining expert workforces, quality review, configurable annotation tools, LLM-assisted labeling, and integrations with ML workflows. An early automated workflow increased the annotation corpus tenfold and tripled annotator productivity.
