Swiggy's pointwise gradient-boosted restaurant rankings could not directly learn relative ordering or balance relevance with delivery objectives. The team explored pairwise neural ranking and a Wide and Deep model with TensorFlow Lattice constraints to combine generalization, remembered preferences, and domain knowledge. The latter architecture improved ROC AUC by 1.86% and PR AUC by 9.25% over the gradient-boosted baseline.
