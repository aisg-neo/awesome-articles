LinkedIn's infrastructure provisioning required repeated coordination between application and infrastructure teams, sometimes taking days or weeks. Nuage evolved from self-service tools for more than 30 infrastructure platforms into a framework for managing the full resource lifecycle. It centralizes resource discovery, access control, policy enforcement, and provisioning workflows.
