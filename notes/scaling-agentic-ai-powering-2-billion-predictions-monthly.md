Salesforce describes moving from predictive and generative AI to Agentforce, which plans and executes multistep enterprise workflows through the Atlas Reasoning Engine. The team added retrieval infrastructure, dynamic inference load balancing, configuration guardrails, and request tracing to support adoption across Salesforce systems. The article reports 1,000 customers in three months and inference pipelines handling up to two billion predictions per month.
