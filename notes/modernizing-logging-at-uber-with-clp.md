Uber extended its CLP-based log compression work into an end-to-end system for viewing, searching, and analyzing unstructured logs. The system lets developers inspect execution sequences and run analytics directly on compressed logs instead of relying on per-host browsing or tools designed primarily for structured events. Earlier streaming-compression integration achieved a 169:1 compression ratio and allowed ten times longer retention.
