Zomato's heuristic restaurant-ad rankings missed customer preferences and context such as mealtime, dietary choices, and affordability. The team formulated promoted-restaurant selection as a learning-to-rank problem using customer, restaurant, and contextual features. It evaluates rankings with metrics including mean reciprocal rank, mean ordered rank, and accuracy at different cutoffs before deployment.
