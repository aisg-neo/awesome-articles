Pinterest improved Actalike audience expansion by combining pretrained user embeddings with per-advertiser multilayer perceptron classifiers. This replaces a blended regression-and-similarity system with one model that handles both sparse user information and advertiser-specific seed lists. End-to-end runtime improved by more than 20%, and an online experiment increased Actalike ad impressions by 3.6% and revenue by 3.1%.
