Razorpay Capital needed to detect duplicate applicants and relationships with blocked merchants as it expanded unsecured credit beyond existing customers. It extended an Elasticsearch-based matching service to compare identifiers such as phone numbers, PAN, and GSTIN across data sources and custom lists. Clear matches can be rejected automatically, while ambiguous cases are presented to underwriters with supporting details.
