Flipkart found that conventional spelling correction failed on English queries typed according to regional Indian pronunciation. It built Sonic, a phonetic transformation and ranking algorithm, and added a sequence-to-sequence model with contextual embeddings to correct whole queries. The combination handles Indian pronunciation patterns and avoids word-level corrections that change a query’s intended meaning.
