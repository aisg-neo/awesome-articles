LinkedIn expanded My Network recommendations beyond people to include entities such as companies, hashtags, groups, and events. It built a second-pass ranking system to compare cohorts from specialized first-pass rankers despite different score scales and value propositions. A/B tests increased engaged members and downstream interactions.
