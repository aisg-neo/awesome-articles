Twitter describes operational challenges after moving its logging service to Splunk Enterprise. It built an API-driven service to generate index and access-control configuration and began replacing fragile modular inputs with plugins on its compute infrastructure. The article also explains log-volume controls and the manual rolling-maintenance process needed to keep large indexer clusters available.
