Notion explains the block-based data model that lets the same content be rearranged, nested, and transformed across different interfaces. Blocks keep their identity, type, properties, and relationships separately, allowing changes in presentation without discarding underlying information. The article connects these design choices to rendering, collaboration, and preserving user intent.
