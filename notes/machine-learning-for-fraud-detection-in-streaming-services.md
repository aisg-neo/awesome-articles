Netflix examines how to detect fraud and abuse across a large streaming platform when reliable labels are limited. It derives features from expected user and device behavior and applies model-based and data-driven anomaly detection to unusual streaming patterns. The article discusses approaches for detecting content fraud, account fraud, and terms-of-service abuse.
