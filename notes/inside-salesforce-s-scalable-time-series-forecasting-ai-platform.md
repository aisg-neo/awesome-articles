Salesforce needed to extend infrastructure forecasting beyond five critical services without proportionally expanding its data science team. It built a configuration-driven time-series forecasting platform that supports different models and use cases. The platform grew to more than 70 forecasting use cases, produces millions of forecasts daily, and reduced new-model deployment time from weeks to days.
