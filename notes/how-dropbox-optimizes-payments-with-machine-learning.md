Dropbox’s fixed retry rules for failed subscription payments had reached a performance ceiling and were costly to maintain. The Payments and Applied Machine Learning teams built a gradient-boosted ranking model to choose promising charge times from customer and payment signals. Moving inference to the shared Predict Service reduced prediction latency from minutes to under 300 ms for 99% of requests.
