Razorpay investigated rising data-platform bills and reduced waste in storage, reporting jobs, and compute clusters. Changes included S3 lifecycle policies, better data locality for merchant reports, cluster reuse, and Trino infrastructure tuning. The combined work saved approximately $2 million per year, with Trino infrastructure costs reduced by more than 60%.
