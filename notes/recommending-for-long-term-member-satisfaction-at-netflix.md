Netflix describes engineering recommendation rewards around long-term member satisfaction rather than directly optimizing clicks or retention. It combines multiple interaction signals into proxy rewards and predicts delayed feedback so recommendation policies can update without waiting weeks for complete observations. The article explains the iterative training and A/B-testing process and how reward misalignment can produce better offline metrics without better member outcomes.
