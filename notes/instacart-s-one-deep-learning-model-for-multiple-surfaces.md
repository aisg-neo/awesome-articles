Instacart's separate XGBoost ad-click prediction models for different browsing surfaces could not share interaction data and increased maintenance work. It replaced them with a unified deep-learning model trained on data from multiple surfaces. The approach supports contextual and high-cardinality features while consolidating training and serving infrastructure.
