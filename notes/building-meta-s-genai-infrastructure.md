Meta built two clusters of 24,576 GPUs to support large generative AI workloads, including Llama 3 training. The article describes their hardware, storage, RoCE and InfiniBand networks, and PyTorch software optimizations. Improvements to process-group initialization reduced startup times from hours to minutes.
