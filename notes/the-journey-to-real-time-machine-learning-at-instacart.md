Instacart moved batch-oriented machine learning toward online inference and real-time features to avoid stale predictions, wasted computation, and poor coverage of new queries or user-item combinations. It introduced a feature store and RPC-based inference platform, then used Kafka and Flink to turn fresh events into features. A unified Griffin interface and cross-functional working groups helped standardize development and coordinate the migration.
