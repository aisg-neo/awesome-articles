PayPal built Cosmos.AI to replace siloed machine learning tools with a unified development, deployment, and operations platform. It covers the model lifecycle from data and feature work through training, serving, monitoring, and governance, and adds infrastructure for generative AI applications. Thousands of employees use its research environments, while hundreds of deployed models serve tens of billions of inference requests daily.
