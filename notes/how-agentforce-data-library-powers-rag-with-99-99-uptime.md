Salesforce’s Agentforce Data Library faced inconsistent retrieval latency and low-confidence results after its general availability launch. The team removed redundant queries, improved retriever prioritization, and added confidence-weighted selection and richer document context. A distributed architecture with asynchronous processing, load balancing, and automated failover supports 99.99% uptime across customer deployments.
