DoorDash unified support chat that had been fragmented across consumers, Dashers, merchants, and client platforms. Shared UI components and an extensible backend preserve conversation context and combine automated support with escalation to human agents. The rollout reduced escalation rates and manual touch points while improving customer satisfaction.
