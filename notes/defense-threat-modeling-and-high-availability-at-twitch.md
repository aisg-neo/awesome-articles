Twitch redesigned availability defenses for a critical client-delivery microservice using threat modeling, layered protection, CDN failover, and capacity planning. The work also improved alerting and service efficiency rather than relying only on redundant instances. Availability rose from 99.9% to 99.99%, alongside a fiftyfold increase in service capacity, lower costs, and better latency.
