eBay combined listing titles and images in a shared embedding system to improve product recommendations and detect mismatched images and text. Its Siamese model uses triplet loss with TransH and a mismatch-detection objective to produce better recall candidates. A/B tests showed increases of up to 15.9% in click-through rate and 31.5% in purchase-through rate.
