Google researchers studied online load balancing when jobs arrive in uniformly random order rather than an adversarial sequence. They derived a stronger lower bound of roughly the square root of log n for the tree-balancing problem and an improved greedy competitive ratio of log m divided by log log m for unrelated machines. The results narrow performance guarantees for scheduling algorithms but leave an open gap between bounds.
