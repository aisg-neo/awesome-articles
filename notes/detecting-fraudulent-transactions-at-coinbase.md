Coinbase built a blockchain address risk-scoring system to detect potentially fraudulent transactions beyond addresses directly associated with its customers. Machine learning combines transaction-pattern features to assign risk scores that can trigger warnings or manual review. The system expanded visibility to millions of external addresses and helped protect user funds.
