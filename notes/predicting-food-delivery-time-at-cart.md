Swiggy needed delivery promises at checkout that balance late arrivals against estimates so conservative they discourage ordering. It built a multi-input, multi-output neural network with entity embeddings to jointly predict interdependent parts of the delivery journey. Shared layers reduced training memory by about 50% and training time to roughly one-fifth, while embeddings reduced overall delivery-time mean absolute error by 5%.
