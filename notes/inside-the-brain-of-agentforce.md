Salesforce describes the Atlas Reasoning Engine that powers Agentforce's enterprise agents. It combines agent state, workflow logic, and actions with an orchestrator and asynchronous, event-driven workflows to interpret requests and execute business tasks. Early pilots reported twice the response relevance and 33% higher end-to-end accuracy than competing or internally built systems.
