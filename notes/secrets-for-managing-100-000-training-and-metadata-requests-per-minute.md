Salesforce describes infrastructure for automating model training, deployment, metadata management, and storage across many customer organizations. Staggered deployments and database tuning reduced rollout time for 140,000 customers from days to hours, while parallel orchestration supported 10,000 training workflows per minute with a 500-millisecond SLA. Metadata caching and validation keep model information available and consistent for inference.
