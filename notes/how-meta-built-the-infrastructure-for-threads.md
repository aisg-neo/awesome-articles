Threads reached more than 100 million signups in five days, requiring Meta’s infrastructure teams to prepare for launch on only two days’ notice. The app reused systems including ZippyDB and Async rather than building dedicated infrastructure. Multitenancy, automated capacity allocation, and online resharding let those services absorb the rapid growth.
