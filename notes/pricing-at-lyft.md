Lyft's pricing system must balance rider demand and driver supply while keeping quotes stable and consistent. Its architecture separates pricing orchestration, operational guidance, and algorithmic pricing, with caching and a traceable quote store supporting execution. Structural pricing optimizes ride-level value while dynamic pricing uses marketplace signals and a near-real-time feedback loop to optimize network throughput.
