Salesforce’s AI Apps team faced retrieval errors, hallucinations, and resource demands from millions of concurrent generative-AI requests. It improved indexing and query embeddings, applied task-specific fine-tuning and user feedback, and monitored output quality. Autoscaling, Kubernetes-based services, regional deployments, and load balancing address throughput and latency requirements.
