Facebook expanded its live-video system to support professional broadcasts with much higher reliability expectations and sudden audience spikes. It added redundant ingestion paths, failover, request coalescing, cache sharding, and additional ISP connectivity. The system supported a peak of 7.2 million concurrent viewers across the Brazil and Spanish-speaking Latin America streams of the UEFA Champions League final.
