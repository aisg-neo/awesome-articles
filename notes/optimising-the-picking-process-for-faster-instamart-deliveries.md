Instamart's one-order-at-a-time picker assignment created backlogs during peak demand at dark stores. Swiggy developed an assignment approach that batches orders with similar items so a picker can collect them together and avoid repeated trips through the store. The article explains the batching logic and validation of its effect on picking efficiency.
