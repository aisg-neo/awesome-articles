PayPal adopted GraphQL to reduce REST overfetching, repeated round trips, and client integration work, beginning with its checkout experience. Adoption expanded through shared infrastructure, training, sample applications, tooling, and a standards group, and GraphQL became the default pattern for new UI applications. The article also describes remaining challenges around consistent error handling, authentication, internal monitoring, and moving independently developed graphs toward a unified gateway.
