Netflix open-sourced Maestro, its horizontally scalable orchestrator for data pipelines and machine-learning workflows. It manages retries, queues, task dispatch, versioned definitions, and reusable patterns such as loops, subworkflows, and conditional branches. After migrating hundreds of thousands of workflows, Netflix reports an average of half a million jobs daily and about two million on busy days.
