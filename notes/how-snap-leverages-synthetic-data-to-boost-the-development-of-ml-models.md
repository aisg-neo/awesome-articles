Snap needed diverse, accurately labeled training data without the cost and limitations of collecting every real-world case. It built a modular synthetic-data generator that augments 3D scenes with assets, textures, facial geometry, lighting, and camera variations. The generated examples provide controllable coverage of underrepresented cases and labels that can be difficult to obtain manually.
