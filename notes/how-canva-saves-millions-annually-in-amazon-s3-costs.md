title: "How Canva saves millions annually in Amazon S3 costs"
link: https://www.canva.dev/blog/engineering/optimising-s3-savings/
topics:
  - Cost Optimization
year: 2023
---

Canva analyzed access patterns and object sizes across more than 230 petabytes of S3 data to identify cheaper storage options. It migrated suitable infrequently accessed content to S3 Glacier Instant Retrieval after accounting for per-object transition fees and minimum billable sizes. The changes preserved millisecond retrieval while saving millions of dollars annually.
