Swiggy found that map providers' car-oriented distances did not reliably reflect routes taken by two-wheeler delivery partners. It synthesized training labels from noisy GPS trajectories and map-based distances, then trained a random-forest model using historical and real-time features. An automated mask for high-error geographic pairs reduced mean absolute error by 10% while sacrificing 3% of trip coverage.
