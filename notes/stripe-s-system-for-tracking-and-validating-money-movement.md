Stripe built Ledger as an immutable, auditable record to standardize money movement across services, banks, and payment networks with different data formats and reporting schedules. Its data quality platform checks fund flows, surfaces discrepancies, and provides investigative tooling. Ledger processes five billion events per day and, together with the platform, provides more than 99.9999% explainability of money movement.
