Spotify needed automated user-growth forecasts across mature and newly launched markets as its business expanded to more than 180 countries. It built weekly and on-demand forecasting pipelines using parallel model tuning, proxy-based forecasts for new markets, quality checks, visualizations, and manual business adjustments. Hyperparameter searches that would take months on one machine now finish within hours.
