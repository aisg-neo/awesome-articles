Coinbase built a notification platform that separates message delivery, content authoring, audience targeting, experimentation, and approval workflows. Its Iris interface lets non-engineers configure and launch personalized notifications across channels. Switching from a managed notification provider to underlying channels reduced costs by more than 50% while handling ten times the workload.
