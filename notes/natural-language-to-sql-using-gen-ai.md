Uber built QueryGPT because writing SQL requires both syntax knowledge and familiarity with internal datasets and business concepts. It combines language models with retrieval of schema information and example queries, refining the retrieval process as the catalog grows. The article estimates that generating a sufficiently reliable query takes about three minutes compared with roughly ten minutes of manual authoring.
