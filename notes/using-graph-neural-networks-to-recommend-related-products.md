Amazon researchers developed a graph neural network for related-product recommendations that models the direction of relationships such as a phone leading to a phone-case purchase. The method assigns separate source and target embeddings and uses a new loss function, while incorporating product metadata to help with cold starts. Experiments improved hit rate and mean reciprocal rank by 30–160% over state-of-the-art baselines.
