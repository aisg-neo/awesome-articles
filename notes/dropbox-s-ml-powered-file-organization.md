Dropbox built smart move to suggest subfolders for unorganized files while letting users accept, reject, or change each suggestion. The team compared a similarity heuristic with a trained model and found that offline accuracy alone did not capture users' organizational preferences. In evaluation, the heuristic achieved 94% accuracy on high-confidence suggestions versus 90% for the trained model.
