LinkedIn developed a multitask learning framework so related AI tasks could share learned representations instead of training isolated models. The framework supports separate datasets, architectures, and losses, addressing differences in label distribution and training requirements. Applications to skills extraction and recruiter search improved model performance and produced statistically significant engagement gains.
