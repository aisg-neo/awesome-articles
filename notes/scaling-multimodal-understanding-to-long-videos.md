Google introduced Mirasol3B to handle long videos without excessively compressing audio and visual information to fit text-oriented multimodal models. It models time-aligned audio and video in chunks, combines them into compact representations, and connects them to a separate text model through cross-attention. The three-billion-parameter model handles 128–512 video frames and improves results on video question-answering and audio-video benchmarks.
