Meta's exabyte-scale analytics platform encountered reliability and efficiency limits with JSON and Hive Text Delimited logging formats. It migrated warehouse analytics logging to the schema-based Tulip serialization format. The article describes the validation, compatibility, and rollout work needed to move deeply integrated systems and reduce logged data volume.
