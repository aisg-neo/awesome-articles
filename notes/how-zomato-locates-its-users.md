topics:

Zomato needed more precise delivery locations than its existing maps and device GPS could provide. It extracted distinctive terms from customer addresses, clustered them geographically, and moderated the resulting building and locality footprints before displaying them on maps. It also classified points of interest to collect structured addresses, reducing delivery-partner calls and handoff time.
