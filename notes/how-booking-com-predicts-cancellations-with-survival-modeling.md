Booking.com’s cancellation classifiers could not adapt quickly to pandemic-driven changes because final training labels were unavailable until check-in. The team applied survival modeling to learn from reservations that had not yet reached their final outcome. This approach handles delayed labels and models when cancellation occurs, rather than treating cancellation solely as a static classification problem.
