DoorDash needed search retrieval to understand compound food queries while enforcing important constraints such as dietary requirements. It used LLMs to segment queries and link them to concepts in its product knowledge graphs, constraining outputs with existing taxonomies. This enriches keyword-based retrieval with contextual understanding while keeping segmentation hallucinations below one percent.
