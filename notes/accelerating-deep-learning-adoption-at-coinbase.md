Coinbase built EasyTensor to simplify deep learning on tabular and multimodal data, where preprocessing and model construction had pushed engineers toward gradient-boosted trees. Its reusable blocks combine feature processing with neural network layers, while Ray handles distributed preprocessing and training. The framework supported datasets 100 times larger and accelerated adoption across teams.
