Uber upgraded its shared search platform from Lucene 7.5.0 to 9.4.2 to support newer capabilities including semantic search. The migration required coordinated Java, Scala, Spark, and Flink upgrades as well as handling incompatible index formats across major versions. The article describes phasing these dependencies and rolling out the library and index changes across serving and ingestion systems.
