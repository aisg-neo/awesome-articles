Razorpay found that overprovisioned Kubernetes resource requests were driving unnecessary EC2 spending across thousands of microservices. After manual rightsizing, it built an Orchestrator with a recommender, scheduled updater, mutating webhook, and visibility tools to automate request tuning while preserving deployment controls. Manual and automated optimization together saved about $300,000 annually.
