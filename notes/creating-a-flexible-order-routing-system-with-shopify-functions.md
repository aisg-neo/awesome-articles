Shopify replaced a fixed order-routing policy with a pipeline of configurable routing rules for selecting fulfillment locations. Rules implemented with Shopify Functions rank locations, and a reducer combines those rankings according to rule priority. Merchants can customize routing around inventory, shipping distance, and business preferences.
