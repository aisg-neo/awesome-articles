DoorDash replaced parts of manual restaurant menu transcription with an OCR-and-LLM pipeline, but varied image quality and menu layouts caused extraction errors. It built an ML guardrail that predicts whether a transcription meets accuracy requirements using image, OCR, and structured features. LightGBM outperformed the more complex models evaluated while also providing the fastest runtime.
