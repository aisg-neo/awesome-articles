LinkedIn migrated its analytics stack from proprietary platforms to open-source big data infrastructure, covering more than 1,400 datasets, 900 data flows, and 2,100 users. Automation helped migrate users and datasets while the team also redesigned data models and pipelines. The transition finished ahead of schedule without negative production impact and saved millions in licensing, support, and operating expenses.
