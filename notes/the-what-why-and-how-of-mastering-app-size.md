Spotify explains how download, installation, storage, and update sizes affect users and how feature growth increases those costs. It integrates size analysis into pull requests and uses metrics and dashboards to detect regressions and guide optimization. In 2022, more than 20 billion Spotify app updates generated over 930 petabytes of network traffic.
