Instacart replaced separate ad hoc retrieval systems with a shared contextual recommendation service for organic and sponsored surfaces. A BERT-like model learns from sequences of product views and cart additions to predict relevant next products during a shopping session. Launching it for cart recommendations produced a 30% lift in user cart additions.
