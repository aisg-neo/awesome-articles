PayPal built JunoDB to provide low-latency key-value storage and caching for services including login, risk, and payments. Its proxy-based architecture combines consistent hashing, replication, quorum-based consistency, and encryption in transit and at rest. The article announces its open-source release and reports 350 billion daily requests with six nines of system availability.
