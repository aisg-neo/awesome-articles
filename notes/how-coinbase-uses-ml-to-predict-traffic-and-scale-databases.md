Coinbase needed to scale databases before traffic spikes because adding capacity after CPU usage rose was too slow. It built a classification model combining platform load with external signals such as cryptocurrency price volatility to predict threshold breaches in the coming hours. The model triggers proactive vertical scaling and scales resources down when demand subsides.
