Lyft needed to measure whether OpenStreetMap accurately reflected roads in its rideshare markets without surveying every intersection. It combined two-stage cluster sampling with Mapillary imagery and curator review to estimate map quality. Core road features were correct more than 95% of the time, while navigation-critical information such as turn restrictions was correct more than 85% of the time.
