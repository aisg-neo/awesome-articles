Spotify describes the collection, processing, and management systems that make up its data platform. Teams define event schemas and pipeline resources while Kubernetes operators provision infrastructure, and shared tools manage orchestration, lineage, access, retention, and quality. The platform collects more than one trillion events daily and supports over 38,000 actively scheduled pipelines.
