Instacart integrated LLMs into search to generate useful substitutes and complementary products beyond direct query matches. Generation combines world knowledge with catalog and behavioral context, then maps suggestions to products and ranks them for discovery surfaces. The team also uses LLM-based evaluation to judge discovery content and reports improved engagement and revenue.
