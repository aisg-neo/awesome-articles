Tinder built Obsidian to address inconsistent UI styling and the growing cost of maintaining its iOS, Android, and web applications. The design system uses base and contextual design tokens managed in Figma with Tokens Studio, then transforms them through Style Dictionary into platform-specific code. The team added a token lookup tool and more than 100 pages of documentation to support migration, with early results showing improved contrast, consistency, and collaboration.
