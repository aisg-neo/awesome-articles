Uber built Odin as a technology-agnostic platform for managing stateful workloads across its own data centers and cloud providers. It uses declarative goal states, independent remediation loops, and Cadence workflows to reconcile placement and lifecycle operations. The platform colocates workloads to optimize CPU, memory, and local-disk allocation, exceeding 95% allocation on the limiting resource.
