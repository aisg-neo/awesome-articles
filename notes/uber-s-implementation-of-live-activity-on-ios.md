title: "Uber's implementation of Live Activity on iOS"

topics:
  - Mobile
year: 2024
---

Uber implemented iOS Live Activities to show pickup and trip information outside the Rider app, including on the lock screen and Dynamic Island. It addressed restricted networking and background execution through shared image caching and updates from the app and push infrastructure. The launch reduced driver cancellations at pickup by 2.26%, rider cancellations by 2.13%, and pickup defects per request by 1.06%.
