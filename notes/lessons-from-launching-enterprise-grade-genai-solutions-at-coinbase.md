Coinbase found that production generative-AI applications required managing latency, capacity, safety, and model changes alongside cost and accuracy. It built CB-GPT as a multi-cloud, multi-model platform with retrieval-augmented generation, evaluation, usage controls, and guardrails. The article describes operational lessons from this platform and the customer-facing chatbot launched in June 2024.
