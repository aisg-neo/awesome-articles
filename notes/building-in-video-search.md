Netflix built in-video search to help creative teams find specific shots or spoken phrases without manually scrubbing through films. The system uses video-text representations, contrastive learning, and a media processing pipeline to retrieve clips from natural-language prompts. Adapting the models improved video retrieval performance by 15–25%, depending on the model and evaluation metric.
