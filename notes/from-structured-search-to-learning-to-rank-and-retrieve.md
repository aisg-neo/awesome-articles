Amazon Music extended learning-to-rank to retrieval because a ranking model cannot recover relevant candidates excluded earlier in the search funnel. The article proposes learning-to-rank-and-retrieve using contextual multiarmed bandits to choose retrieval strategies from customer feedback. It explains how balancing exploration and exploitation addresses limitations of static structured and neural retrieval approaches.
