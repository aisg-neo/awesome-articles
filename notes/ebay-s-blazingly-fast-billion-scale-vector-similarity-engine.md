eBay built an approximate nearest-neighbor similarity engine for item similarity and personalized recommendations across its marketplace. Sharding, replication, pluggable ANN backends, and attribute filtering make vector retrieval work at billion-record scale. Its index of 1.7 billion active listing embeddings returned 200 nearest neighbors within 12 milliseconds at the 95th percentile.
