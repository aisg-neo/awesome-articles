Instacart's independent text and semantic retrieval systems fetched fixed numbers of products, which could over-fetch irrelevant items or miss the best mix for a query. The team built hybrid retrieval that uses query entropy to adapt how the two methods contribute to the candidate set. The design accounts for differences in query specificity and retailer assortments instead of merging independently tuned result lists. The change improved mean converting position by 1.7% and reduced latency by 1.5%.
