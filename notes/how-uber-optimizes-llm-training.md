Uber needed to adapt open-source language models to its domain knowledge while making training practical at its scale. It built an internal training stack using on-premises A100s and cloud H100s together with libraries for distributed training, fine-tuning, evaluation, and inference. The article explains how open-source tools and in-house infrastructure support continuous pretraining and instruction fine-tuning for applications such as Uber Eats.
