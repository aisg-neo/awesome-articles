Netflix developed a foundation model for recommendations to reduce the maintenance burden of many independently trained personalization models. It learns from large-scale content data and members' longer interaction histories, then shares those learnings through embeddings or weights for fine-tuning. This centralizes preference learning so multiple recommendation tasks can reuse the same representations.
