Snap built Bento to unify feature generation, training, model management, and deployment for its server-side machine learning workloads. The platform supports petabyte-scale training, more than 500 deployed models, and over a billion predictions per second. Local feature hydration and optimized Protobuf deserialization cut dataplane latency in half and made it ten times cheaper.
