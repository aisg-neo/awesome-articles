Flipkart personalized search autosuggestions using customers' recent searches to predict what they are likely to seek next. The article describes ranking features, model evaluation, and work to align feature distributions between training and inference. Service-flow and Solr feature-lookup optimizations helped the system meet tight latency requirements.
