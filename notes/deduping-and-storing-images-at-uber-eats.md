Uber Eats built a content-addressable caching layer to avoid repeatedly downloading, processing, and storing duplicate product images. The problem grew as grocery and other catalogs reused images across many stores. The system was developed and fully rolled out in less than two months, improving image-service latency and reliability while reducing processing and storage costs.
