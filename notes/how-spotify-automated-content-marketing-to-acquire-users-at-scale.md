Spotify built an automated acquisition system to generate content-based ads, publish them, measure performance, and update content selection. It combined scalable creative rendering with an XGBoost ranking model trained on campaign, artist, and template data. In two regional experiments, the model lowered cost per registration by 4% and 14% compared with its heuristic predecessor.
