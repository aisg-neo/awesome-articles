Amazon researchers use large teacher language models to generate labeled examples for smaller conversational AI models when real training data is scarce. LINGUIST creates controlled, annotated utterances for intent classification and slot tagging, while CLASP generates semantic-parsing examples across languages. The methods improved multilingual intent and slot benchmarks by 2–4 absolute points and semantic parsing by 5–6 points.
