title: "World scale inverse reinforcement learning in Google Maps"

topics:
  - AI/ML
year: 2023
---

Google scaled inverse reinforcement learning to learn routing preferences from observed travel across a world-sized road network. Graph compression, parallelization, and Receding Horizon Inverse Planning make training tractable, while offline reward computation and online graph search keep serving efficient. The resulting policy improved global route match rates by 16–24% relative to the existing baseline.
