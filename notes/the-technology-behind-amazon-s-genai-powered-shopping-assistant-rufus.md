Amazon built Rufus as a shopping assistant using a custom language model trained on its catalog, reviews, community questions, and public web information. Retrieval-augmented generation supplies current evidence, and customer feedback supports reinforcement learning. Trainium and Inferentia optimizations, continuous batching, and token-by-token streaming reduce response delays while formatting and hydrating answers with shopping data.
