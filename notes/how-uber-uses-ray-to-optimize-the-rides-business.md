Uber's rides marketplace optimization needed to parallelize Python model training and budget-allocation calculations that were inefficient in its Spark-based workflow. It adopted Ray for those computations while retaining Spark where it suited data processing. The allocation tuning system achieved speedups of up to 40 times and faster development iterations.
