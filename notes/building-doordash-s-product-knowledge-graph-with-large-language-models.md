DoorDash used large language models to standardize and enrich inconsistent merchant SKU data that had previously required costly manual extraction and auditing. The pipelines extract product attributes and discover new brands, using retrieved examples and additional information to improve coverage and precision. Better organic-label coverage enabled targeted product carousels that improved engagement metrics.
