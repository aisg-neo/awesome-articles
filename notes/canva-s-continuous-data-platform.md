Canva replaced its existing analytics ingestion path with Snowpipe Streaming as data growth increased cost and freshness demands. The team describes integrating the streaming client and managing throughput, correctness, and operational behavior. The rollout reduced overall platform cloud spending by 45% and brought time-to-query latency to approximately ten minutes.
