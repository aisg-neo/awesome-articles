Instagram added text styles and animations to Stories and Reels while managing Android app size and differences in text rendering across platforms. The team addressed font delivery, glyph positioning, and ligatures so animations could operate correctly across varied scripts. The update also introduced a consistent style picker and decorated variants for every text style.
