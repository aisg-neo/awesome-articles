Lyft built Verity to centralize data-quality definitions, validation, and results for its Hive warehouse. Checks run as data is created and before release, with alerts and historical results helping teams diagnose failures. The platform supported more than 70 teams and 2,500 tables, performed 140,000 validations per week, and had prevented over 13,000 incidents.
